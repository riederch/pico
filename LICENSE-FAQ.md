# License FAQ

This FAQ is a practical explanation of Pico's licensing intent. It is not a replacement for `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` or `COMMERCIAL.md`.

Pico is source-available for private and non-commercial use under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 with Pico-specific additional permissions.

Commercial use requires prior written permission from the designated Pico rights holder.

Current commercial permission contact:

```text
https://github.com/riederch
```

## What is allowed without commercial permission?

The following uses are allowed without additional commercial permission:

- private use by one person
- private Pico Home use
- private Pico WG use
- family or household use
- private shared-home use
- private non-commercial friend-group use
- hobby, study, experimentation and testing without anticipated commercial application
- private cost sharing that only reimburses actual shared infrastructure costs

## What is a private Pico WG?

A private Pico WG is a non-commercial shared Pico Home arrangement between people who personally share a household, family context, friendship context or similar private relationship.

A private Pico WG may share actual infrastructure costs such as electricity, storage or server hosting costs.

It must not become a paid hosting service, subscription, managed service, support business, SaaS offering or commercial service.

## Can a family use one Pico Home together?

Yes.

Family and household use is allowed as private non-commercial use.

## Can a shared flat use one Pico Home together?

Yes.

A private shared home may use Pico as a private Pico WG, including private cost sharing for actual infrastructure costs.

## Can friends run a shared Pico Home?

Yes, if it is private and non-commercial.

It must not be advertised as a public service, paid hosting, managed hosting or subscription.

## Can a club, volunteer fire brigade or non-profit use Pico?

This is not automatically commercial, but it can be context-dependent.

Non-commercial testing, evaluation, training or hobby use is generally intended to be allowed.

Operational production use by an organisation should ask for permission first, especially if Pico is used for official duties, member services, infrastructure operation, data processing, paid work or organisational administration.

When in doubt, ask for permission.

## Can a school, university or research group use Pico?

Non-commercial study, teaching, research and experimentation are intended to be allowed.

Commercial research, sponsored product development, paid services, internal business operations or deployment as an organisational service require permission.

## Can a company test Pico internally?

A company may inspect and evaluate Pico for non-production testing.

Business-internal use, production use, use for employees, use for customers, use in commercial workflows, or use as part of a commercial service requires prior written permission.

## Can an IT service provider install Pico for someone?

If the provider is paid for installing, operating, supporting or maintaining Pico, permission is required.

Helping a friend privately and without compensation is allowed as private non-commercial use.

## Can someone offer paid Pico hosting?

No.

Paid Pico hosting, managed Pico Home services, Pico Home rental, multi-tenant Pico Home hosting for money and SaaS operation require prior written permission.

## Can someone offer paid support?

No, not without permission.

Paid support, support bundles, managed operation and commercial consulting around Pico require prior written permission.

## Can someone accept donations?

Donations can be a grey area.

Occasional voluntary donations for a private non-commercial project may be acceptable if they do not create paid access, paid support, hosting obligations, subscriptions, service tiers, business advantage or profit.

Donation-funded public hosting, support obligations, paid tiers or service-style operation require permission.

## Can someone make videos, tutorials or articles about Pico?

Non-commercial tutorials, reviews and educational material are allowed.

Paid courses, commercial training, sponsored content, commercial consulting funnels or bundled support offerings may require permission.

## Can someone fork Pico?

Private and non-commercial modification is allowed within the license.

Compatibility claims are separate from licensing. A fork must not present itself as official Pico, official Pico Home, Pico Home Managed or Pico-compatible unless the applicable compatibility, naming and provenance requirements are met.

## Can a fork remove license restrictions?

No.

Forks must comply with the license and required notices.

## Does Pico grant trademark rights?

No.

The license does not grant trademark rights, brand rights, endorsement rights or official-service rights.

## Does Pico compatibility grant commercial permission?

No.

A compatible implementation still needs commercial permission for commercial use.

Compatibility is about protocol behaviour and user safety. It is not a commercial hosting license.

## What should I do if I am unsure?

Ask before using Pico commercially or organisationally.
