# Commercial Use

Pico is source-available for private and non-commercial use, and for business use on a self-operated Pico Home.

Commercial Pico hosting remains reserved to the designated Pico rights holder. Any other commercial use requires prior written permission.

## Permitted without commercial permission

The following uses are permitted without additional commercial permission:

- private use by an individual
- private Pico Home use
- private Pico WG use
- family or household use
- private shared-home use
- private non-commercial friend-group use
- hobby, study, experimentation and testing without anticipated commercial application
- private cost sharing that only reimburses actual shared infrastructure costs
- business use of one or more Picos on a self-operated Pico Home

Private cost sharing must not create profit, paid hosting, paid support, a subscription, a managed service, a commercial service, or a business advantage.

## Self-operated business use

A business, organisation, association or other legal entity may run one or more Picos on its own Pico Home without asking for commercial permission. A guesthouse, workshop, practice, farm, club or small company can put Picos to work for itself, and those Picos may interact with its owners, members, employees, guests and customers.

The condition is that the Pico Home is self-operated.

A Pico Home is self-operated when the entity that uses it:

- holds custody of that Pico Home's host keys,
- operates and controls that Pico Home itself, and
- does not obtain that Pico Home from a third party as commercial hosting, a managed service or part of a paid offering.

Renting general-purpose infrastructure — a server, virtual machine, storage or connectivity — is not by itself commercial Pico hosting, as long as the entity remains the only party that operates the Pico Home and holds its host keys.

A third party may be paid to install, configure, migrate, maintain, update or support a self-operated Pico Home on behalf of the entity that operates it. That third party must not operate the Pico Home as its own service, hold the host keys as a service provider, or offer Pico Homes to its own customers.

## Interaction is not provision

The line between permitted business use and reserved commercial hosting is not whether outsiders come into contact with Pico, and not whether money is involved. It is whose companion is being run.

Interaction is permitted. Guests, customers and other people outside the entity may interact with a Pico that the entity operates, and their own Picos and Pico Homes may communicate with it. This holds even when that interaction is part of what those people pay for. A guesthouse Pico that answers guest questions is the entity's own Pico doing the entity's work.

Provision is reserved. An entity provides Pico when another party gets a Pico or a Pico Home of their own that the entity operates, or of which the entity holds the host keys, so that the entity runs a companion on that party's behalf. Enrolling people outside the entity as members of a Pico Home the entity operates counts as provision.

Provision for money, or as part of a paid offering, is commercial Pico hosting and requires prior written permission.

## Commercial permission required

Prior written permission is required for commercial use that is not covered by self-operated business use, including but not limited to:

- paid Pico hosting
- managed Pico Home services
- Pico Home rental
- multi-tenant Pico Home hosting for money
- operating a Pico or a Pico Home on behalf of another party as a service or as part of a paid offering
- SaaS operation
- selling Pico or Pico-based products
- giving parties outside your own entity a Pico or a Pico Home of their own that you operate, as a product, as a service or as part of a paid offering
- integration into a commercial product or service
- redistribution or sublicensing for money
